Essay structure

Section names and word ranges as in the brief. Total: 1,000 words. Going more than 10% above or below (under 900 or over 1,100) will likely affect your grade. The reference list and AI Declaration are not counted.

SectionWordsJob of this section
1. Initial Evaluation of AI Response150-200Your first impressions: what seems convincing, what seems questionable, and how you will verify it.
2. Analysis of Strengths and Weakness of AI Response450-500Critically analyse the response using your Verification Record: what can and cannot be verified, biases, overgeneralisations, unsupported claims, and omissions.
3. Evaluation of Your Verification Process150-200Explain and evaluate how you checked the claims, how you chose your sources, and what difficulties you met.
4. Verification Record150-200A table: at least five claims, at least five different academic sources, each with evidence and a verdict.
5. Reference Listnot countedSources in APA, alphabetical order.
6. AI Declarationnot countedYour own confirmation that you used no AI in any part of this assessment.
Suggested order of work. Write section 1 first, as honest first impressions, before you start checking. Then research and fill in the Verification Record. Section 2 and 3 are easier once the record is filled in, because section 2 must use the material in it. The builder keeps the brief's order; you can work in any order.

How your essay is assessed

The brief's rubric has four criteria: Structure (required sections, linking words, structure that helps your argument), Content (relevant content and sources, a well-reasoned argument, critical thinking), Referencing (in-text citations and reference list present, complete and matching), and Formatting and Writing (clear writing, few errors, correct formatting). Read the full rubric at the end of your brief.

The universal paragraph formula

Use this in any paragraph that makes a judgement, adapting it to the job of the paragraph.

1. PointWhat am I claiming?
2. EvidenceWhat supports it? Which source?
3. ExplanationWhat does the evidence mean?
4. LinkHow does this connect to my judgement of the AI response?
Every judgement you make about the AI response must be traceable to evidence in your Verification Record. If you cannot point to the evidence, check again or rethink the judgement.

Essay builder

For each paragraph: answer the questions in your own words, press Put my answers in the draft box, then edit the result into a real paragraph. New here? Open Guide in the steps bar first. Section 4, the Verification Record, is a table in its own step.

Word count

4. Verification Record

150 to 200 words. You must verify at least five claims and use at least five different academic sources. This table counts in your word total.

For each row: copy the claim from the AI response; give the details of the academic source or sources you used; give evidence from the sources that supports or disagrees with the claim; and choose a verdict: Accurate, Partially accurate, or Inaccurate.

5. Reference List and 6. AI Declaration

Neither is counted in the word total. The reference list comes first, then the AI Declaration, both at the end of your essay.

Your declaration must confirm that you have not used AI in any part of this assessment, including research, planning, finding sources, writing and editing. Write it yourself.
